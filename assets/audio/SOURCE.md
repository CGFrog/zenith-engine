# Audio asset provenance

Both files in this directory are original synthetic assets generated for
Zenith on 2026-09-25. They contain no sampled or third-party source material.

- `interaction.wav`: a 120 ms, 880 Hz sine tone with a linear decay, mono,
  48 kHz, 16-bit PCM.
- `ambient.ogg`: a four-second mono chord made from 110, 165, and 220 Hz sine
  waves with short fades, 48 kHz, encoded as Ogg Vorbis.

They may be used and redistributed under the same terms as the Zenith project.
